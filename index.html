WHY "STEWARD"

A steward is the person a shop owner trusts to run everything when he isn't looking. He keeps the books. Tracks the stock. Remembers who owes what. Never sleeps, never forgets, never steals. He doesn't own the shop — he makes sure it runs.

That's exactly what this software is.
